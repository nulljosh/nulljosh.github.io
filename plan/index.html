Finance Track — SFU

CS is the base. Finance is the multiplier — it's what turns a dev job into quant/fintech/trading pay. Two ways to add it, pick one at end of year 2:

OptionWhat it costsBest if
Finance minor
Beedie, ~7 courses inside the BSc		 No extra years, no extra tuition beyond normal course load. Needs BUS 217/312 + electives. You want to graduate on time and work as a dev at a fintech/bank.
MSc Finance
Beedie, ~12–16 months post-BSc		 ~$35–45k tuition, one extra year of no salary. Needs decent GPA + GMAT/GRE. You want quant / portfolio / trading roles, not just engineering.

Default: take the minor. It's free relative to the degree and keeps the master's open — MSc Finance admits CS grads, and a Finance minor + CS BSc is a stronger application than CS alone. Decide on the master's after you've worked 1–2 years and know whether you actually want the quant side.

Practical note: keep first-year calc and stats strong — both the minor and MSc lean on them, and it's the same calc you're already taking to keep engineering open. No extra prereq cost.

Step 0 — KPU Upgrading (Do First)

Checked your transcript: you graduated 2018 with Pre-Calculus 11 (C+) — satisfies the BC grad requirement, but SFU Computing Science needs Pre-Calculus 12. That's the one gap. English is already covered (English 12 C+, English Studies 12 = A), so no English upgrading needed.

Pre-Calc 12 is free at KPU via Math Qualifying (MATQ) — e.g. MATQ 1130. It's continuous intake, so you're not late — apply now and start before Fall term.

Cross-checked against SFU's official CS requirements: your English Studies 12 (87%), Science 12 (Anatomy & Physiology 12 / Biology 12), and Law 12 already cover the "5 approved Grade 12 courses" rule. Pre-Calc 12 is the only mandatory piece left — nothing else required. Source: SFU CS admission requirements

Optional: Head-Start Path (not required)

Simplest route: Pre-Calc 12 only (free) → apply as a BC high school completer. If you want a jumpstart instead, KPU also offers real for-credit Computer Science courses — CPSC 1150 or 1155 (intro programming) — that transfer directly to SFU as CMPT credit. Trade-off: they cost tuition, and taking them shifts your SFU application from the simple "high school completer" pathway into a "transfer student" pathway (needs 24+ transferable credits, competitive GPA) instead of the Grade 12 course list above. Stick with just Pre-Calc 12 unless you specifically want to bank a first-year course early.

Disability Funding — Apply Before SFU Starts

  • CSG-D: up to $2,800/yr, tuition/books. Via StudentAid BC.
  • CSG-DSE: up to $20,000/yr, equipment/tutoring only. Via school Accessibility Coordinator.
  • StudentAid BC verification is separate from PWD, but your PWD medical evidence should speed it up.

Other Programs Worth Checking

  • RDSP — up to $3,500/yr grant + $1,000/yr bond, free money that compounds. Open one early.
  • GST/HST Credit — automatic once you file taxes, keep filing every year.
  • CWB disability supplement — kicks in once you have co-op/part-time income.
  • Community Living BC (CLBC) — supports for adults with autism diagnosis, worth a conversation.
  • PWD health supplements — dental, equipment, some therapy costs. Ask your worker what's covered.
